
1. The practice of connecting computers and other devices to share resources and information.
2. It enables communication, resource sharing, and centralized data management across devices.
3. B. Sharing of data and resources between computers**
4. A network is a group of interconnected devices; the **internet** is a global system of interconnected networks.
5. The internet is a collection of networks connected together using standardized protocols.
6. Email communication
   File sharing and cloud storage
   Web browsing and online research
7. Bus: All devices share a single communication line.
   Star: All devices connect to a central hub or switch.
   Ring: Each device connects to two others, forming a circular pathway.
8. B. Star
9. Advantages:High fault tolerance
              Direct connections offer high performance
   Disadvantages:Expensive due to many cables
                 complex installation and maintenance
10.LAN(Local Area Network): Covers a small area like an office or building.
   MAN(Metropolitan Area Network): Covers a city or large campus.
   WAN(Wide Area Network): Covers large geographical areas, often worldwide.
11.LAN — because it is cost-effective, easy to set up, and sufficient for limited-area networking needs.
12. D. WAN
13.Router: Connects different networks and directs data packets.
   Switch: Connects devices within a LAN and manages data flow.
   Hub: Broadcasts data to all connected devices (less efficient).
   Modem: Converts digital signals to analog for internet access and vice versa.
14. A switch sends data only to the intended device; a **hub** broadcasts data to all devices.
15. True
